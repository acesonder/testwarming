🏠 Homeless Outreach Platform

Welcome to the Homeless Outreach Platform - a comprehensive system designed to assist outreach staff, service providers, and people experiencing homelessness. Our platform helps coordinate services, track outreach efforts, and connect individuals with the resources they need.

👥 User Management

Secure registration and authentication for staff, outreach workers, and service providers.

🤝 Outreach Coordination

Tools to help coordinate outreach efforts and track engagement with individuals in need.

📊 Service Tracking

Monitor and document services provided to ensure comprehensive support.

🔒 Secure & Private

Built with security and privacy in mind to protect sensitive information.
