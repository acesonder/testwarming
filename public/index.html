🏠 Homeless Outreach Platform

Welcome to the Homeless Outreach Platform - a comprehensive system designed to assist outreach staff, service providers, and people experiencing homelessness. Our platform helps coordinate services, track outreach efforts, and connect individuals with the resources they need.

👥 Client Management

Comprehensive client profiles with contact information, demographics, and case history.

📊 Service History Tracking

Track and document all services provided to clients including outcomes and follow-ups.

💼 Case Management

Manage cases with goals, action plans, and priority tracking for better outcomes.

💬 Instant Messaging

Secure messaging between outreach staff and clients for better communication.

📝 Assessment Forms

Comprehensive assessment tools for intake, mental health, housing, work, income, and addiction.

✅ Consent Management

Digital consent agreements with signature tracking and witness documentation.

🔐 Role-Based Access

Secure authentication with admin, outreach, staff, and provider roles.

🔒 Privacy & Security

Built with data protection and privacy compliance in mind.
